## Capital, time and economics
Route-wide flash principal cap <=15% of eligible route-wide TVL floor, subject to stronger adapter/lender constraints. Enforce BUY_EXECUTABLE < SELL_EXECUTABLE for simple two-venue cases; for all cycles enforce actual end-token output greater than input plus flash premium, gas, slippage, bribes and buffers before execution. C1 and C2 are cycles, not legs. C2 only follows successful C1 receipt, re-reads state, is idempotent and respects its bounded post-receipt window; a reverted C1 yields NO_OP. Per-stage deadlines must be derived from measured RPC, fork, simulation and transaction durations, not arbitrary global 3-second ceilings. Provider fabric remains fail-closed with >=1 verified healthy configured provider and scored redundancy.
